Report abuse or objectionable content

Open the profile of the user in question, tap the ••• menu, and choose Report or Block. Reporting also hides that person from you. We review every report within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that violate our Terms.

Delete your account

Go to Account → Delete account. This permanently erases your profile, recommendations, reactions, watchlist, favorites, and connections. It can't be undone.

Common questions

How do I add a friend? Tap the add-a-binger button in your circle and enter their email. If they're not on WUW+ yet, they'll connect with you automatically once they join.

I'm not getting notifications. Make sure notifications are enabled for WUW+ in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that you're not in a Focus mode.

Where does the title info come from? From TMDB, Apple, and TheTVDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
