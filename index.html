Hi, my name is Fernando Abreu, and I've spent the last 10 years at Microsoft as a Principal Tech PM. I began my career as a software engineer, then moved into consulting to help customers adopt Agile practices and apply SDLC/PMO methodologies to run their IT transformation and Business Innovation programs more effectively. From there, I was invited to build a Modern Applications professional services practice from the ground up leading a group of 22 people at peak.

My time at Microsoft has been centered on delivery excellence in the Azure Cloud&AI space. Rooted in a software‑engineering background, I take projects from Idea to Design and stay technically savvy enough to drive Code to Launch — increasingly through an FDE / Applied AI delivery model. I also specialize in lead‑to‑order and deal shaping strategy, helping executives shepherd the approval and delivery of Microsoft's largest and most intricate engagements, all while running global programs to strengthen and streamline tools and methods with a strong appetite for process automation and “kaizen” (continuous improvement).

And that's just my side hustle — my full‑time job is being a proud girl dad, loving husband, and distinguished pet parent. I'm originally from Brazil, spent a chapter as a French Canadian, and later became a naturalized American now living in beautiful, historic Richmond, Virginia.
